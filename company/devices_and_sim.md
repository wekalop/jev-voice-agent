# Devices and SIM

All amounts are in Egyptian pounds and include taxes.

## SIM replacement

- A replacement SIM costs fifty pounds at any store. It takes about ten minutes.
- The customer must bring their original national ID. A photo is not accepted.
- A faulty SIM is replaced free.
- Only the account holder can replace a SIM. A representative needs a formal power of attorney, and must go to a store.
- Courier delivery adds forty pounds, and takes two working days. It is available only in Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria.
- The courier checks the national ID at the door.
- The assistant can never replace a SIM by phone.

## Lost or stolen phone or SIM

- The assistant can block the line at once, after level one identity checks. Blocking is free.
- A blocked line keeps its number, and postpaid plan fees continue while it is blocked.
- A store visit with the national ID is needed to get the line back.
- If the customer thinks someone else took over their number, it is treated as fraud. Pass the caller to a human right away.

## eSIM

- An eSIM costs thirty pounds.
- Postpaid customers can set it up in the Nabda app. It takes about twenty minutes.
- Prepaid customers must go to a store the first time.
- The code works once. A new phone needs a new eSIM, at thirty pounds.
- eSIM is not available for Home Air routers.
- The Kestrel A3 does not support eSIM. The Orbit Mini and Orbit X5 do.

## Moving your number to Nabda

- Moving a number to Nabda is free.
- The customer needs their national ID, and can start in the app or at a store.
- It takes up to three working days. The line may be down for up to two hours during the move.
- The number must have no unpaid bills with the old network.
- A number can move only once every ninety days.
- Balance on the old network does not move across.

## Moving your number away from Nabda

- A human agent issues the transfer code. The assistant cannot.
- The code is valid for seven days.
- All postpaid bills must be paid first.
- Remaining device installments stay due, even after the number leaves.
- Prepaid balance is lost when the number moves.
- Moving away is the customer's right. Do not try to stop them.

## Devices sold on installments

- Kestrel A3: six thousand pounds.
- Orbit Mini: nine thousand six hundred pounds.
- Orbit X5: eighteen thousand pounds.

### Twelve-month plan, no extra fee

- Kestrel A3: five hundred pounds a month.
- Orbit Mini: eight hundred pounds a month.
- Orbit X5: one thousand five hundred pounds a month.

### Twenty-four-month plan, with a ten percent fee

- Kestrel A3: two hundred seventy-five pounds a month.
- Orbit Mini: four hundred forty pounds a month.
- Orbit X5: eight hundred twenty-five pounds a month.

## Installment rules

- Only postpaid customers can buy on installments. Prepaid customers cannot.
- The customer must be at least twenty-one, and must have been with Nabda for six months.
- The account must have no overdue bill.
- A customer can have two devices on installments at most.
- The first payment appears on the next bill after delivery.
- A missed installment is treated like any unpaid bill, with the same late fee and bar.
- Paying off early has no penalty, but the ten percent fee is not returned.
- An unused device in a sealed box can be returned within fourteen days.
- Devices come with a one-year warranty. Warranty claims are handled at stores.
- Device orders are taken in stores and in the app. The assistant can explain the rules, but cannot place the order.

## Unlocking

- Devices on installments are locked to Nabda until the last payment.
- After the last payment, the unlock is free and takes up to three working days. It happens automatically.
- Paying off early also starts the unlock.
- Devices bought outright, with no installments, are never locked.
- Nabda cannot unlock a phone bought from another company.
