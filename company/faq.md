# Frequently asked questions

## How do I check my balance and data?
Dial star one five five hash. You can also check in the Nabda app. It shows your balance, data left, and renewal date.

## What are your cheapest plans?
The cheapest is Go Week, at forty pounds for seven days. The cheapest monthly plan is Go Mini, at seventy pounds.

## What does Go Smart include?
Go Smart costs one hundred thirty pounds for thirty days. You get eight gigabytes and three hundred minutes.

## What does Flex Gold include?
Flex Gold costs four hundred seventy-five pounds a month. You get sixty gigabytes and two thousand minutes. Up to ten gigabytes of unused data carries over.

## Who can get Go Youth?
Go Youth is for customers aged sixteen to twenty-five. We check your age against your national ID.

## What happens to Go Youth when I turn twenty-six?
Your line moves to Go Smart on the first renewal after your birthday. The price changes to one hundred thirty pounds.

## Does unused data roll over?
Usually not. The exceptions are Go Plus, Flex Gold, and Boost Month. Each has its own limit.

## What happens when my data runs out?
On most plans, your data stops until you renew or buy a bundle. Flex Platinum and home fiber slow down instead of stopping.

## What is Auto Boost?
It is an optional setting for postpaid lines. When your data runs out, it buys Boost Day for you, up to three times in a billing cycle.

## What is Boost Night?
Boost Night costs twenty-five pounds for seven days, and gives you ten gigabytes. It only works from one a.m. to seven a.m.

## Does Boost Social cover video calls?
No. It covers chat and social apps, but voice and video calls use your main data.

## Can I add a bundle to my home internet?
Home Air plans can add Boost Month only. Home fiber plans cannot add any bundles.

## How much does fiber installation cost?
It costs five hundred pounds once. It is free if you sign a twelve-month contract.

## What is the difference between Home Air Lite and Home Air Pro?
Home Air Lite uses four G, with speeds up to thirty megabits per second. Home Air Pro uses five G, with speeds up to three hundred megabits per second, and works only in five G areas.

## Can I take my Home Air router to another address?
No. The router only works at your registered address.

## When is my bill due?
Your bill is due fourteen days after your bill date. You get three extra days of grace before a late fee.

## How much is the late fee?
The late fee is thirty pounds for each unpaid bill. It is added on the fourth day after the due date.

## What happens if I do not pay my bill?
On day fifteen, outgoing calls and data stop. On day thirty, the line is suspended. On day ninety, the line is ended and you could lose the number.

## How do I get a suspended line back?
Pay everything you owe, plus a fifty pound reconnection fee. Your line comes back within two hours of the payment posting.

## How can I pay my bill?
You can pay in the Nabda app, on the website, at a store, or at a partner kiosk. Kiosks charge a five pound fee. I can also text you a secure payment link.

## Can I pay by card over the phone?
No, I can't take card details by voice. I can text you a secure payment link instead.

## Can I pay my postpaid bill with a recharge card?
No. Recharge cards only work for prepaid lines.

## Can I get a refund if I overpaid?
Yes. It becomes credit on your next bill by default. If you prefer, it can go back to your original payment method within ten working days.

## I bought a bundle by mistake. Can I get my money back?
Yes, within twenty-four hours, if you have used less than fifty megabytes of it.

## How do I dispute a charge?
Tell me which charge it is, within sixty days of the bill date, and I'll open a ticket. The review takes up to ten working days. You still need to pay the rest of the bill.

## Can I get a paper bill?
Yes, for ten pounds a month. Bills in the app are free.

## When does a plan change start?
A postpaid move up starts right away, and a move down starts on your next bill date. A prepaid move up starts right away, and a move down starts on your renewal date. Home internet changes start on your next bill date.

## Can I move from postpaid to prepaid?
Yes, if you owe nothing and have no active device installment.

## Is there five G in my area?
Five G is in parts of Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria, and in Sakkala in Hurghada. Tell me your area and I'll check.

## How do I turn on roaming?
Use the Nabda app, or dial star one five five star seven hash. Do it before you leave Egypt, because it can take up to one hour.

## How much does roaming cost?
Daily passes are one hundred twenty pounds in Zone One, one hundred ninety pounds in Zone Two, and two hundred eighty pounds in Zone Three. Tell me the country and I'll check its zone.

## Will I get a huge bill from roaming?
We have limits to protect you. Pay-as-you-go data stops at one hundred pounds a day unless you agree to continue. Postpaid roaming stops at one thousand pounds per billing cycle.

## Does a roaming pass work on a cruise ship or a plane?
No. Ship and plane networks cost much more, so turn roaming off or use airplane mode on board.

## How do I replace my SIM?
Bring your original national ID to any store. It costs fifty pounds, and it's free if the SIM is faulty. I can't replace a SIM by phone.

## I lost my phone. What do I do?
I can block your line right now, for free, after a quick identity check. You'll need to visit a store with your national ID to get the line back.

## How much is an eSIM?
An eSIM costs thirty pounds. Postpaid customers can set it up in the app. Prepaid customers need to visit a store the first time.

## Can I keep my number if I move to Nabda?
Yes, it's free. It takes up to three working days, and your number can't have unpaid bills with your old network.

## How do I move my number away from Nabda?
A human agent gives you a transfer code, valid for seven days. Your postpaid bills must be paid first. I can connect you now.

## Can I buy a phone on installments?
Yes, if you are on a postpaid plan, are at least twenty-one, and have been with us for six months. Your account can't have an overdue bill.

## When will my phone be unlocked?
It unlocks after your last installment, for free. It takes up to three working days, and it happens automatically.

## What are your store hours?
Most stores open from ten a.m. to ten p.m., Saturday to Thursday, and from one p.m. to ten p.m. on Friday. Luxor and Aswan close at nine p.m. Ramadan hours are different.

## When can I talk to a person?
Human agents are available from eight a.m. to midnight, every day. After midnight, I can book a callback for the next morning.

## How long does a complaint take?
We acknowledge it within twenty-four hours, and answer most complaints within five working days. If it takes longer, it goes to the complaints manager.

## Do I get compensation for an outage?
If your home internet is down for more than twenty-four hours in a row, you get one day of your plan fee back as bill credit. It's automatic. Mobile outages have no automatic credit.

## Are you a real person?
No, I'm Nabda's virtual assistant. I can connect you to a person if you'd like.

## Can my husband or wife call about my account?
No. Only the account holder can get account details by phone. A representative needs a formal power of attorney, at a store.
