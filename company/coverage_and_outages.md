# Coverage and outages

This information is correct as of Tuesday, the third of November 2026, at nine a.m. Cairo time.

## Coverage by city

Four G means normal mobile data. Five G and fiber are available only in the areas named.

### Cairo
- Four G covers all of Cairo.
- Five G: Nasr City, Heliopolis, New Cairo, Maadi, and Downtown.
- Fiber: Nasr City, Heliopolis, New Cairo, Maadi, Mokattam, Rehab, and Madinaty.

### Giza
- Four G covers all of Giza city, Sheikh Zayed, and Sixth of October.
- Five G: Dokki, Mohandessin, Sheikh Zayed, and Sixth of October.
- Fiber: Dokki, Mohandessin, Sheikh Zayed, and Sixth of October.

### Alexandria
- Four G covers the whole city, including Borg El Arab.
- Five G: Smouha and Sidi Gaber.
- Fiber: Smouha, Sidi Gaber, and Miami.

### Mansoura
- Four G covers the city and Talkha.
- There is no five G.
- Fiber: Mansoura University District only.

### Tanta
- Four G covers the city.
- There is no five G and no fiber.

### Ismailia
- Four G covers the city and the canal road.
- There is no five G and no fiber.

### Hurghada
- Four G covers the city and the main coastal road.
- Five G: Sakkala only.
- There is no fiber.

### Luxor and Aswan
- Four G covers the city centers.
- Outlying villages may get only slower service.
- There is no five G and no fiber. Home Air Lite is the only home plan here.

### Assiut
- Four G covers the city.
- There is no five G and no fiber.

### Sharm El Sheikh
- Four G works in the city, through a shared network.
- There is no Nabda store, no five G, and no home internet.

### Limited or no coverage
- Service is unreliable on open desert roads and in the Western Desert oases.
- For any town not listed here, the assistant should not guess. It should say coverage is not confirmed, and suggest the coverage map in the Nabda app.

## Compensation for outages

If home internet is down for more than twenty-four hours in a row, the customer gets one day of the plan fee back as bill credit.
This credit is automatic, and appears on the next bill. It does not need a ticket.
Mobile outages have no automatic credit.
For anything else, follow the compensation limits in the support policy.

## Basic troubleshooting

### Phone has signal but no data
- Check that data has not run out, by dialing star one five five hash.
- Turn airplane mode on, wait ten seconds, then turn it off.
- Restart the phone.
- Check that mobile data is turned on.
- If it still fails, open a ticket.

### Phone has no signal
- Check for an outage in the caller's area.
- Restart the phone.
- Try the SIM in another phone, if one is handy.
- A faulty SIM is replaced free at a store.

### Fiber router
- A green light means working.
- A blinking green light means starting up. Wait three minutes.
- A red light means no connection from the street. Check for an outage, then check that the thin fiber cable is not bent or loose.
- Unplug the router, wait thirty seconds, then plug it back in.
- If the red light stays on, open a ticket for a technician.
- A technician visit is free when the fault is on Nabda's side. If the customer's own cable or device caused the fault, the visit costs one hundred fifty pounds.

### Home Air router
- Place it near a window.
- Check that it is at the registered address. It will not work anywhere else.
- Unplug it, wait thirty seconds, then plug it back in.
- A faulty router is replaced free within the first twelve months.
