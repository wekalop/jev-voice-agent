# Billing policy

All amounts are in Egyptian pounds and include taxes. Say "pounds" when speaking.

## Prepaid lines

Prepaid customers do not get bills.
The plan fee comes out of the balance on the renewal date.
If the balance is too low on the renewal date, the plan pauses. The line can still receive calls, but it has no data and cannot make outgoing calls.
The plan waits seven days for a recharge. After that, the line moves to pay-as-you-go minute rates with no plan.
Balance expires if the customer does not recharge for one hundred eighty days.
Data cannot be bought from the balance without a plan or bundle.

## Postpaid billing cycle

Each postpaid customer has a bill date on the first, the tenth, or the twentieth of the month.
The customer picks the bill date when signing up, and can change it once every twelve months.
Home internet is the exception. All home-internet bills are issued on the first of the month.
The plan fee is charged in advance. Extra usage is charged for the month just ended.

## Due dates

A bill is due fourteen days after the bill date.
A customer who pays by the due date pays no fee.
There is a three-day grace period after the due date.

## What happens if a bill is not paid

Days are counted from the due date.

- Day four: a late fee of thirty pounds is added. This is charged once for each unpaid bill.
- Day fifteen: the line is partly barred. Outgoing calls and data stop, but incoming calls still work.
- Day thirty: the line is fully suspended. New monthly plan fees stop while the line is suspended.
- Day ninety: the line is ended, and the number can be lost. The debt moves to the collections team, which is human only.

## Getting a line back

The customer must pay everything owed, plus a reconnection fee of fifty pounds if the line was fully suspended.
There is no reconnection fee after a partial bar.
The line comes back within two hours of the payment posting.
A payment plan may be possible, but only the human billing team can discuss it.

## Late fee waiver

The assistant may remove one late fee, if the customer has had no other late fee waived in the last twelve months.
The caller must pass level two identity checks first.
A waiver does not cancel the rest of the bill, and it does not lift a bar until the bill is paid.

## Payment methods

- The Nabda app, by bank card or Nabda Wallet.
- The website, by bank card.
- Automatic bank card payment, set up in the app. There is no discount for this.
- Partner payment kiosks, in cash. Kiosks charge a five pound service fee, and the payment can take up to two hours to post.
- Any Nabda store, by cash or card, with no fee.
- Prepaid recharge cards work for prepaid lines only, and cannot pay a postpaid bill.

The assistant never takes card details by voice. It can send a secure payment link by text message.

## Refunds

- An overpayment becomes credit on the next bill by default.
- A customer can ask for the overpayment back instead. It goes to the original payment method within ten working days.
- Working days are Sunday to Thursday.
- Prepaid recharges cannot be refunded. The one exception is a double recharge at a kiosk, reported within seven days with the receipt. A human handles this.
- A bundle bought by mistake can be reversed within twenty-four hours, if less than fifty megabytes of it has been used.
- Plan fees are not refunded for part of a month when a customer cancels.
- Fiber installation fees cannot be refunded after the technician has visited.

## Disputes

A customer can dispute a charge within sixty days of the bill date.
After sixty days, only a supervisor can consider it.
The assistant logs the dispute and gives the ticket number.
The review takes up to ten working days, and the result comes by text message.
No late fee is charged on the disputed part while it is under review.
The rest of the bill must still be paid by the due date.
If the dispute succeeds, the amount comes back as bill credit.
If the dispute fails, the customer has fourteen days to pay the disputed part.

## Plan changes and billing

- A postpaid move up starts immediately. The customer pays the price difference for the days left in the cycle.
- A postpaid move down starts on the next bill date.
- A prepaid move up starts immediately, and the full new plan fee comes from the balance. Any data left on the old plan ends.
- A prepaid move down starts on the next renewal date.
- A home-internet plan change starts on the next bill date, whether it is up or down.
- Moving from postpaid to prepaid requires no money owed and no active device installment.
- Moving from prepaid to postpaid costs nothing, but must be done at a store with a national ID.

## How to read a bill

A bill has these parts, in this order.

- Summary: the total due and the due date.
- Plan fee: the monthly price of the plan, for the coming month.
- Add-ons: any bundles bought during the month.
- Extra usage: minutes beyond the plan and international calls.
- Roaming: passes and pay-as-you-go roaming charges.
- Device installment: the monthly payment and how many are left.
- Late fees: any late fees added.
- Payments and credits: what has already been paid or credited.

Bills arrive by text message with a link, and can be seen in the Nabda app for free.
A paper bill costs ten pounds a month.
