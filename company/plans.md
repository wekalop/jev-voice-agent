# Nabda plans

All prices are in Egyptian pounds and include taxes. There is nothing to add on top.
When speaking, use the line labeled "Say". Say "pounds", not "EGP".

## General rules

- A plan's minutes cover Egyptian numbers. International calls are charged separately.
- Unused data does not roll over, except on Go Plus, Flex Gold, and Boost Month.
- Bundle data is used first. Rollover data is used next. Main plan data is used last.
- Prepaid and postpaid data stops at the limit, except on Flex Platinum, which slows down instead.
- Minutes beyond the plan cost one pound fifty per minute on prepaid, and one pound per minute on postpaid.
- Auto Boost is an optional postpaid setting. When data runs out, it buys Boost Day automatically, up to three times per billing cycle.
- Boost bundles add to a mobile plan. They cannot be used on home fiber at all. Home Air plans accept only Boost Month.
