# About Nabda

Nabda is a mobile and home-internet operator in Egypt. The name is said NAB-da.
Nabda has about two million subscribers. About one million eight hundred thousand are mobile customers, and about two hundred thousand are home-internet customers.
Nabda has forty retail stores.

## What Nabda sells

- Prepaid and postpaid mobile plans
- Add-on data and minutes bundles
- Home fiber and four G or five G home internet
- International roaming
- Device installment plans

## History

- Two thousand twelve: Nabda started in Alexandria as a small home-internet provider.
- Two thousand sixteen: Nabda launched mobile service in Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria.
- Two thousand nineteen: Nabda launched home fiber in Cairo.
- Two thousand twenty-two: Nabda passed one million subscribers.
- Two thousand twenty-four: Nabda launched five G in parts of Cairo and Alexandria.
- Two thousand twenty-five: Nabda opened its fortieth store, in Assiut.

## Contact channels

- Customer care from a Nabda line: dial one five five. The call is free, including while roaming.
- Customer care from any other phone: 02 5550 0155, said as oh two, five five five zero, oh one five five. Normal call rates apply.
- Roaming desk from abroad: +20 2 5550 0177, said as plus two zero, two, five five five zero, oh one seven seven. It is open twenty-four hours. The caller's local call rates apply.
- Self-service code: star one five five hash. It shows balance, data left, and renewal date.
- The Nabda app: bills, bundles, recharge, roaming, and in-app chat.
- Website: nabda dot example.
- Email for general help: care at nabda dot example.
- Email for formal complaints: complaints at nabda dot example.

## Opening hours

All times are Cairo time.

- The voice assistant on one five five is open twenty-four hours, every day.
- Human agents are available from eight a.m. to midnight, every day.
- After midnight, the assistant can open a ticket and book a callback for the next morning.
- In-app chat with a person follows the same hours as human agents.
- Stores open Saturday to Thursday, from ten a.m. to ten p.m.
- On Friday, stores open from one p.m. to ten p.m.
- Stores in Luxor and Aswan close at nine p.m. every day.
- During Ramadan, all stores open from ten a.m. to four p.m., and again from eight p.m. to midnight.
- All stores close on the first day of each Eid.
- Home technicians visit between nine a.m. and nine p.m., and not on the first day of each Eid.
